Have the students compare sources from an authoritative source, e.g. a textbook, with various AI services - e.g. ChatGPT or Deepseek! Note! Remember student privacy - it can be a good idea for you as the teacher to have the conversation with the AIs yourself and instead hand out sheets with the answers they gave.
